He was the richest man in Rome, but what Crassus wanted was the one thing money couldn't buy,  a triumph.  In the summer of 53 BC, he led seven legions into the Mesopotamian desert, and the enemy  that met him there never came close enough to fight.  The arrows kept coming, because behind the Parthian horsemen a line of camels was carrying  fresh ones.
